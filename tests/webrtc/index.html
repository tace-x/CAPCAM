Important boundary: these controlled pages own the generated MediaStream themselves. A successful page-to-page clone or local RTCPeerConnection test does not prove that the production offscreen document can deliver its MediaStream to an arbitrary website. The Chrome extension/page boundary requires its own verified mechanism.
